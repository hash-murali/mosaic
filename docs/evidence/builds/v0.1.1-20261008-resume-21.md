## Signing feasibility and next acceptance

Apple documents that data-protection Keychain access entitlements must be
[authorized by a provisioning profile](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3137-on-mac-keychains).
Xcode documents [Personal Teams for non-members](https://help.apple.com/xcode/mac/current/en.lproj/dev60b6fbbc7.html).
These establish an account/signing setup requirement; they do not prove that a
Personal Team supports this exact Mac target and capability. Paid membership is
not asserted mandatory without capability verification. Owner must configure a
supported team in Xcode before native acceptance; account setup is not performed
by the agent. Preserve user-presence/device-only protection; no weak fallback.

Scoped audit here covers synthetic process durability, unchanged source identity,
current compilation/test outcomes and release-gate accuracy. It is not a final
security assessment. Broader content-first design stays v0.1.2 per D-020.
